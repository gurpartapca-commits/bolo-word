    1How it works

    1. Press the mic and allow microphone access when your browser asks.
    2. Speak naturally. Pause briefly between sentences.
    3. Fix anything by typing, then copy or download. You can keep talking while you edit.

    Punjabi vocabulary: about 76,000 words from Bhai Kahan Singh Nabha's Mahan Kosh, plus any words you add. Works best in the latest Chrome or Edge, online. Your speech is processed by the browser's speech service; this page keeps your text only on your device.

    2Say the punctuation

    Turn off “Spoken punctuation” if you need these words written out.

    3Tips for better results

    • Use a headset or keep the mic 15–20 cm from your mouth.
    • Quiet room, steady pace, clear endings.
    • Check names, places, numbers and English words afterwards.
    • The result is a first draft. Read it through before you share it.
